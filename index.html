    How it works

    From search to paid in one thread.

    No duplicate spreadsheets. No re-entering the same request. Every step updates the same record.

    1. 01

      Plan the semester

      Students search the catalogue, compare sections and see seats, schedules and prerequisites before committing.

    2. 02

      Validate and submit

      Four rules run instantly. Anything that doesn't fit is explained in plain language, with the fix in view.

    3. 03

      Approve, then pay

      Advisors decide from one queue; invoices and statuses update automatically on the student's account.
