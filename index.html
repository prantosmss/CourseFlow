    One system, three ways in

    Each role signs in with an ID and password and lands on its own dashboard (FR01).

    Student

    Registers each semester.

    • Search offered courses
    • Register, drop or join a waitlist
    • View schedule and status
    • Pay the invoice online

    Faculty advisor

    Approves advisees' choices.

    • See pending registrations
    • Approve in one click
    • Reject with a required remark

    Administrator

    Registrar's office staff.

    • Manage courses and offerings
    • Set seat capacity and dates
    • Create and disable users
